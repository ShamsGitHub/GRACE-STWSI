# GRACE-STWSI

R scripts for analysing and visualising the Standardised Terrestrial Water
Storage Index (STWSI) derived from GRACE satellite observations.

## Overview

This repository contains R code supporting the GRACE-based terrestrial water
storage analysis developed for **Indicator 1.2.2: Drought** in *The 2026 Report
of the Lancet Countdown on Health and Climate Change*.

The analysis uses terrestrial water storage (TWS) observations from the Gravity
Recovery and Climate Experiment (GRACE) and GRACE Follow-On satellite missions
to characterise extremely dry and wet terrestrial water storage conditions and
long-term changes in water storage. GRACE dataset used in this analysis comes
from CSR GRACE/GRACE-FO RL06.3 Mascon Solutions (https://www2.csr.utexas.edu/grace/RL0603_mascons.html).

## Standardised Terrestrial Water Storage Index (STWSI)

The **Standardised Terrestrial Water Storage Index (STWSI)** was derived from monthly terrestrial water storage (TWS) observations from the **CSR GRACE/GRACE-FO Mascon product**. Missing monthly observations were filled using linear interpolation for short data gaps. For the extended GRACE–GRACE-FO mission gap during 2017–2018, missing monthly values were replaced using the corresponding monthly climatological means calculated over the six-year period from January 2015 to December 2020. The resulting continuous monthly TWS time series was then standardised relative to the long-term monthly climatology to produce the STWSI.

Extremely dry conditions are defined as:

`STWSI < -1.5`

and extremely wet conditions as:

`STWSI > 1.5`

For the Lancet Countdown analysis, changes in the frequency of extremely dry
and wet conditions were assessed by comparing **2006–2015** with **2016–2025**.

The analysis showed spatially heterogeneous drying and wetting patterns across
the globe. An additional 16% of global land area experienced at least one month
of extremely dry conditions, while an additional 14% experienced extremely wet
conditions between the two periods.

## Terrestrial water storage trends

Long-term changes in terrestrial water storage were also assessed from GRACE
observations for 2003–2025. Approximately 43% of global land area experienced
decreasing water storage of at least 1 mm/year, while 37% experienced an
increase.

The analysis includes estimation of terrestrial water storage trends using the
Theil-Sen estimator.

## Repository structure

- `R/` – R scripts used for the STWSI country-level analysis
- `data/` – information on the input datasets and data availability
- `outputs/` – outputs generated by the analysis

## R script

`R/STWSI_country_analysis.R`

The script calculates changes in the frequency of extremely dry terrestrial
water storage conditions between 2006–2015 and 2016–2025 and aggregates the
results at country level.

## Data

The analysis uses GRACE and GRACE Follow-On terrestrial water storage
observations. The large monthly STWSI dataset is not stored directly in this
GitHub repository.

Further information on the input data is provided in `data/README.md`.
