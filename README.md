# GRACE-STWSI

R scripts and supporting data for analysing and visualising terrestrial water
storage conditions derived from GRACE and GRACE Follow-On satellite observations.

## Overview

This repository contains R code and supporting datasets for the GRACE-based
terrestrial water storage analysis developed for **Indicator 1.2.2: Drought**
in *The 2026 Report of the Lancet Countdown on Health and Climate Change*.

The analysis uses monthly terrestrial water storage (TWS) observations from the
Gravity Recovery and Climate Experiment (GRACE) and GRACE Follow-On (GRACE-FO)
satellite missions to characterise extremely dry and wet terrestrial water
storage conditions and long-term changes in water storage.

The GRACE dataset used in this analysis is the **CSR GRACE/GRACE-FO RL06.3
Mascon Solution**, produced by the Center for Space Research (CSR), University
of Texas at Austin:

https://www2.csr.utexas.edu/grace/RL0603_mascons.html

## Standardised Terrestrial Water Storage Index (STWSI)

The **Standardised Terrestrial Water Storage Index (STWSI)** was derived from
monthly TWS observations from the CSR GRACE/GRACE-FO Mascon product.

Missing monthly observations were filled using linear interpolation for short
data gaps. For the extended observational gaps during 2017–2018, missing
monthly values were replaced using the corresponding monthly climatological
means calculated over the six-year period from January 2015 to December 2020.
The resulting continuous monthly TWS time series was then standardised relative
to the long-term monthly climatology to produce the STWSI.

The STWSI dataset covers **January 2003 to December 2025**.

Extremely dry terrestrial water storage conditions are defined as:

`STWSI < -1.5`

and extremely wet conditions as:

`STWSI > 1.5`

For the Lancet Countdown analysis, changes in the frequency of extremely dry
and wet conditions were assessed by comparing **2006–2015** with **2016–2025**.

The analysis showed spatially heterogeneous drying and wetting patterns across
the globe. An additional 16% of global land area experienced at least one month
of extremely dry terrestrial water storage conditions, while an additional 14%
experienced at least one month of extremely wet conditions between the two
periods.

## Terrestrial water storage trends

Long-term changes in terrestrial water storage were assessed using GRACE and
GRACE-FO observations for **2003–2025**. Approximately 43% of global land area
experienced decreasing water storage of at least 1 mm/year, while 37% exhibited
an increase.

Long-term TWS trends were estimated using the **Theil-Sen estimator**, a
non-parametric method for estimating the magnitude of temporal trends.

## Repository structure

- `R/` – R scripts used for the STWSI and terrestrial water storage analyses
- `data/` – supporting datasets and information on data availability
- `outputs/` – outputs generated by the analysis

## R script

`R/STWSI_country_analysis.R`

The script:

- calculates changes in the frequency of extremely dry conditions between
  2006–2015 and 2016–2025;
- calculates corresponding changes in extremely wet conditions;
- extracts country-level Theil-Sen trends in terrestrial water storage for
  2003–2025; and
- produces country-level results and a map of changes in extremely dry
  terrestrial water storage conditions.

Country-level analyses use the country list and geographical classifications
developed for the Lancet Countdown 2026 analysis.

## Data availability

The monthly global STWSI raster:

`CSR_GRACE_Global_Standardised_TWSI_03_25.tif`

is archived in the **UCL Research Data Repository (Figshare)**.

The dataset is currently under repository review. A private-access version is
temporarily available at:

https://figshare.com/s/e216d66641b4f9481c94

The permanent UCL Research Data Repository DOI will be added here once the
dataset is published.

The smaller supporting datasets required by the R script, including the
Theil-Sen TWS trend raster, Lancet Countdown country information, country
boundaries, and Caspian Sea mask, are provided in the `data/` directory.

To reproduce the analysis, download
`CSR_GRACE_Global_Standardised_TWSI_03_25.tif` from the UCL Research Data
Repository and place it in the `data/` directory before running the R script.

Further information on the datasets and processing is provided in
`data/README.md`.
